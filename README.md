# First Semester: An Arts Story

**Find your way. Meet people. Explore your options.**

*First Semester: An Arts Story* is an interactive narrative game set on TU Dublin’s Grangegorman campus. You play as a new BA Arts (TU980) student navigating the first semester: finding buildings, meeting other students and staff, exploring subjects and making decisions about your academic pathway.

The game was developed to bring the early student experience to life. It introduces academic advising, module choice and the opportunities of the Arts programme through conversations and situations that students may encounter, rather than through instructions alone. There is room to explore, change your mind and ask for help.

## Play the game

**[Play First Semester: An Arts Story](https://shaunferns.github.io/an_arts_story/geo/)**

Start at the Broadstone–University Luas stop and make your way around Grangegorman. Follow the story, speak to people and use your in-game phone to check **What’s next**, messages and the campus map. Your progress is saved in your browser. Turn on **Sound** to hear the surroundings change as you walk from the Luas to East Quad.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W / S** or ↑ / ↓ | Walk forwards / backwards |
| **A / D** or ← / → | Step left / right |
| **Space + WASD** or drag | Look around |
| **Shift** | Run |
| **E** | Talk or enter when prompted |
| **M** | Open or close the phone |
| **Esc** | Close a panel or skip the arrival |
| **V** | Switch between first- and third-person views |

## About the project

The BA Arts programme offers students opportunities to explore different disciplines and shape a pathway through a Major, optional Minor, Subject Area Requirements and Free Electives. Those possibilities also bring questions about prerequisites, timetables, capacity and future choices.

The game uses storytelling and a walkable campus to help make these questions more familiar. It is intended to complement the guidance and conversations provided by the BA Arts team and Academic Advisers.

The people and situations in the game are fictionalised. Module choices and timetables are illustrative; actual programme decisions and registration are managed through TU Dublin’s official processes.

## Development and feedback

The game continues to develop, particularly its campus representation, building detail and narrative experience. Feedback from BA Arts students and staff is welcome through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ShaunFerns/an_arts_story/issues).
