# World locations

The campus model reconstructs an existing physical campus. Compare architectural drawings, site plans and verified photographs with the current implementation before changing it. Preserve existing geometry, location, terrain, routes and interactions except where a source identifies a specific error. Document fictional details, simplifications and estimated dimensions.

World coordinates are east and north in metres relative to the Rathdown House reference. Three.js uses east for X, height for Y and negative north for Z. The normal phone map is north-up. Its 1,000 × 1,000 drawing at the default zoom uses `mapX = 500 + 0.925 × (east − 112)` and `mapY = 500 + 0.925 × (−45 − north)`. Zoom and recentering use that same projection with a different scale and centre.

## Entrance and route conventions

- Approaches use the original projected campus. Door interactions are placed on the pedestrian side of the mapped wall. Central Quad’s doorway uses the clearer section of the courtyard frontage. **E** at a door opens the location panel. **E** at the outdoor contact starts the relevant conversation. The semester story uses exterior meetings at Central Quad, East Quad, Lower House, Rathdown House and the Academic Hub & Library. The archived route starts with Bríd at East Quad, then sends players to Aoife at Central Quad and Cian at Lower House.
- The existing east–west route, East Quad opening orientations, north entrance frontage and Rathdown origin remain in use. The access-road deck bridges the Broadstone cutting with a provisional 9 m clearance. The original four-metre terrain rise from the Luas stop towards East Quad is unchanged. Mapped stairs and paths follow the prototype’s graded walking surface.
- The library doorway is on the playing-fields side. The dated OSM construction fence still encloses that approach. The game adds a 5.6 m pedestrian gap and a short paved approach, with the original source snapshot retained. The gap and door position are provisional. [Academic Hub and Library opening](https://www.ggda.ie/what-we-do/news/academic-hub-library-officially-opened-on-september-11th-2026/)
- Central Quad uses pale precast surrounds projecting around broad bays of glass, with brick infill and stepped flat roof levels. The photo references show two L-shaped blocks arranged around the courtyard. The roof height breaks are estimated on the mapped footprint. [Feilden Clegg Bradley Studios project photographs and plans](https://www.archdaily.com/978001/central-quad-grangegorman-campus-feilden-clegg-bradley-studios)
- The Academic Hub & Library uses roughcast infill, Kilkenny limestone coloured plinth and projecting sills, a glazed entrance beneath an overhang and upper bands of narrow windows. The roof and terrace heights distinguish its south and inferred north volumes while keeping North House visibly separate. [Architectural Association of Ireland plan and site visit](https://architecturalassociation.ie/aai-site-visit-grangegorman-odonnell-tuomey-architects/) · [Library façade reference](https://www.mfsgroup.com/project/tu-dublin-academic-hub-grangegorman/) · [Exterior photograph](https://www.kilsaran.ie/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Grangegorman-Academic-Hub-and-Library.pdf)
- The retained buildings have their own colours and masonry details. Rathdown and North House use grey stone with pitched slate. Lower House has warmer ashlar, a hipped roof and vertical sash windows. The Clock Tower uses a surveyed west elevation and separate ranges. See the [Clock Tower comparison](clock-tower-review.md). Glassmanogue and Bradogue use greyer masonry and retain their original proportions. St Laurence’s has its slate gable, buttresses and pointed lights. The surviving stone walls near these buildings share the local masonry palette. [TU Dublin campus building photographs](https://www.tudublin.ie/explore/our-campuses/grangegorman/buildings-on-campus/) · [An Croí restoration](https://www.ggda.ie/what-we-do/projects/an-croi/) · [Lower House restoration](https://purcell.ie/projects/grangegorman-lower-house/)
- The library and Lower House footprints are provisional attributions in the snapshot. The latter uses the feature labelled Sports and Student Facility. Entrances, roof levels, façade dimensions, kerb detail, underpass alignment and elevations remain candidates for on-site review. [TU Dublin access map](https://www.tudublin.ie/media/website/explore/our-campuses/city-centre-campuses/grangegorman/documents/00GC-DDS-GEM-ZZ-VS-GD-XX-0002-WayfindingMapCampus.pdf)

## Entrance and paving review

The DXF aligns closely to the footprints and corroborates the courtyard and turf layout. The door and window geometry in the game comes from the exterior photographs and plans described below. The positions and dimensions remain estimates in game metres.

- **St Laurence’s** has gabled porches near the south end of the nave. Both porches project from the nave and have pointed stone surrounds and recessed timber leaves. The existing interaction uses the eastern porch. The steep nave roof, lower north chancel and sacristy, and bellcote on the north gable are separate forms. Dimensions are estimated from the footprint, aerial imagery and photos. [NIAH chapel record](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50070358/saint-laurences-chapel-saintbrendans-hospital-grangegorman-lower-dublin-7-dublin)
- **Central Quad** retains the courtyard-side game entrance. Its position is an estimate informed by the plan and courtyard photographs. The glazing now has side lights and an overlight. The approach uses pale slabs with warm inlays and leaves the planted centre of the court. [Architects’ project plans and photographs](https://www.archdaily.com/978001/central-quad-grangegorman-campus-feilden-clegg-bradley-studios)
- **Academic Hub & Library** uses the playing-fields side entrance described by TU Dublin. The glazing and sidelights sit beneath the limestone-coloured overhang. [TU Dublin Library directions](https://ittdublin.ie/library/location-and-opening-hours/grangegorman/) · [Kilsaran project photographs](https://www.kilsaran.ie/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Grangegorman-Academic-Hub-and-Library.pdf)
- **Lower House**, **Rathdown House** and **Glassmanogue** use the available entrance photographs for the proportions of their doors, stone surrounds and sash details. The model uses pale setts and flags around their thresholds. The exact position at Glassmanogue remains provisional. ## Rathdown forecourt and Broadstone arrival

- The Rathdown reception is in the east wall of the projecting wing, facing into the court between that wing and the canted pavilion. The TU Dublin and GDA photographs show the perpendicular return beside it and a second doorway with a small timber hood on that return. Figure A2a.8 and the aerial view establish the building wings and roof directions. The southern tip of the projecting wing is a separate gable end. The door offsets are estimated from the photographs. The ramp starts separately at the Grangegorman Lower gateway, east of the court. Its rails follow OSM ways 1355378351–1355378353. The paved court broadens past the ramp and retains the central tree island. The 0.66 m rise over about 11.6 m is an estimate for the game. [Planning Scheme, Figure A2a.8](https://ggda.ie/media/a3vnvuc2/13_gg-appendix_2a.pdf#page=19) · [TU Dublin entrance photograph](https://arrow.tudublin.ie/gggal/13/) · [Mapped gate railing](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1355378351)
- At Broadstone–University the supplied photographs informed the setts, concrete retaining walls, bench, lighting, glazed bridge parapet and Grangegorman lettering. The bridge and route heights remain provisional. The Broadstone Station masonry façade sits above the platform wall.
- The Glassmanogue photograph informed the blue door panels and brick window heads. [TU Dublin historic buildings](https://www.tudublin.ie/media/grangegorman/documents/Historical-Buildings.pdf)
